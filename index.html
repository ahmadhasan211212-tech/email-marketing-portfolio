Email marketing that doesn't feel like marketing

I'm Ahmad Hasan — an email marketer who treats every campaign like a conversation, not a broadcast. I build sequences that respect the reader, deliver value first, and convert naturally.

My Approach

Strategy → Copy → Design → Optimize

1. Strategy First. I map your customer journey, segment your list, and plan flows that make sense for your business.

2. Copy That Converts. Subject lines people open. Body copy people read. CTAs people click. No fluff.

3. Design That Renders. Responsive templates that look great in Gmail, Outlook, and every client in between.

4. Measure & Iterate. Every send is a learning opportunity. I track, report, and continuously improve.
