About Automate Sends

Email marketing that doesn't feel like marketing

We're Automate Sends — an email marketer who treats every campaign like a conversation, not a broadcast. I build sequences that respect the reader, deliver value first, and convert naturally.

From welcome flows to re-engagement win-backs, we've designed, written, and optimized email programs for SaaS, e-commerce, and B2B clients across the globe.

Our Approach

Strategy → Copy → Design → Optimize

1. Strategy First. We map your customer journey, segment your list, and plan flows that make sense for your business.

2. Copy That Converts. Subject lines people open. Body copy people read. CTAs people click. No fluff.

3. Design That Renders. Responsive templates that look great in Gmail, Outlook, and every client in between.

4. Measure & Iterate. Every send is a learning opportunity. I track, report, and continuously improve.
