How to Set Up a Welcome Flow in Klaviyo (Step-by-Step)

July 8, 2026 · 12 min read · By Ahmad Hasan

A welcome flow is the highest-ROI automation you can build. New subscribers are at peak engagement — they just opted in. If you don't capture them now, you lose them forever.

This guide walks through building a 4-email welcome flow in Klaviyo that consistently converts at 12%+. No fluff, just the steps.

Step 1: Create the Flow

In Klaviyo, go to Flows → Create Flow → Browse Ideas → search "Welcome Series." Choose the 3-email template as a starting point. We'll expand it to four.

Step 2: The Trigger

Set the trigger to "Subscribed to List" and select your main list. Important: add a flow filter so existing subscribers don't re-enter — "Has never been in this flow."

Step 3: Email 1 — Immediate (0 min delay)

The Deliverable. This email fulfills whatever promise got them to sign up (discount code, lead magnet, free resource). Keep it short. One goal: deliver value fast.

  • Subject: "Here's your [promised thing] 🎉"
  • Body: Deliverable front and center + one soft brand intro sentence
  • CTA: Use/download the deliverable

Step 4: Email 2 — 24 Hours Later

The Story. Share your brand story or founder journey. Build emotional connection. People buy from brands they relate to.

  • Subject: "The story behind [brand name]"
  • Body: Origin story, mission, values — written like a human, not a press release
  • CTA: Browse bestsellers or explore the site

Step 5: Email 3 — 48 Hours Later

The Proof. Social proof + product education. Show them what others are buying, reviews, before-and-afters.

  • Subject: "What our customers are saying"
  • Body: 2–3 testimonials or case studies, product spotlight
  • CTA: Shop the featured products

Step 6: Email 4 — 72 Hours Later

The Offer. Time-boxed incentive to purchase. This is where conversion happens.

  • Subject: "Your welcome gift expires tomorrow ⏳"
  • Body: Reminder of the welcome offer + urgency + bestsellers
  • CTA: Shop with discount

Step 7: Test & Optimize

Once live, create an A/B test on Email 1 — try two different subject line types from the 7-framework. Wait for 2,500 sends per variant. Keep the winner, test Email 2 next. Repeat.

Pro tip: Add a conditional split after Email 1 — if they purchased, skip the rest and move to a post-purchase flow. No point selling to someone who already bought.

Set this up once and it runs forever. The welcome flow for my skincare client generated 22% of their monthly revenue — on autopilot.
