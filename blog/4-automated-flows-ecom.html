Beyond Welcome: 4 Automated Flows Every eCom Brand Needs

April 25, 2026 · 9 min read · By Ahmad Hasan

Most eCom brands have one automated flow: welcome. That's leaving 70% of potential revenue on the table. Here are four more flows that run on autopilot and generate revenue while you sleep.

1. Browse Abandon Flow

Trigger: viewed product page, didn't add to cart. Wait 2 hours, then send a soft nudge. "Still thinking about [product]? Here's why people love it." Include 2–3 reviews and a direct link back. This flow alone adds 3–5% incremental revenue.

2. Cart Abandon Flow (3 emails)

  • 1 hour: "You left something behind" — product image + one review
  • 24 hours: "Still available in your size" — add social proof, answer FAQ
  • 48 hours: "Last chance + free shipping" — escalate the incentive

Add a conditional exit: if they purchase at any point, remove them from the remaining emails.

3. Post-Purchase Flow

This is the most underrated flow in eCom. After delivery confirmation, send:

  • Day 3: Usage tips / care instructions (value, not selling)
  • Day 10: "Customers who bought X also love Y" — cross-sell
  • Day 30: Review request + loyalty program mention

4. Win-Back Flow

Trigger: no purchase in 90 days. Three emails:

  • Email 1: "We noticed you've been quiet" — highlight what's new since their last purchase
  • Email 2: "We miss you — here's 15% off" — soft incentive
  • Email 3: "Last email from us" — clear unsubscribe option, sincere goodbye

The third email sounds counterintuitive, but it works. People who don't want your emails will unsubscribe — that's a good thing for deliverability. Those who stay but don't buy get suppressed from promos but kept for lifecycle emails.

Set these up once. Maintain them quarterly. Watch your baseline revenue climb with zero additional ad spend.
