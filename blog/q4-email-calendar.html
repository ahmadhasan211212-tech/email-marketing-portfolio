The Ultimate Q4 Email Calendar: What to Send and When

May 12, 2026 · 10 min read · By Ahmad Hasan

Q4 is make-or-break for email marketers. November and December alone drive 30–50% of annual email revenue for most eCom brands. If you're not planning by September, you're already behind.

October: Prep Phase

  • Week 1: List cleaning — suppress inactive subscribers, verify deliverability
  • Week 2: Early-access teaser — "Something big is coming"
  • Week 3: Gift guide email — staff picks, curated by price range
  • Week 4: Halloween angle (if relevant) + sneak peek at holiday collection

November: BFCM Month

  • Early Nov: VIP early access — your top 10% get first dibs
  • Mid Nov: Gift guide 2.0 + "Shop early to avoid shipping delays"
  • Week of BF: 5-email sequence: teaser Mon → early access Wed → BF main Fri → reminder Sat → last chance Sun
  • Cyber Monday: Separate angle — digital products, subscriptions, gift cards

December: The Long Game

  • Week 1: Shipping deadline emails — "Order by Dec 15 for guaranteed delivery"
  • Week 2: Last-minute gift ideas + gift card push
  • Week 3: Post-holiday sale teaser
  • Week 4: Year in review + "What's coming in 2027"

Key Principle: Send Less, Not More

During BFCM week, inboxes are flooded. Your 5-day sequence shouldn't exceed 1 email per day. Quality + timing beats volume.

Map this calendar now, write the emails in September, and Q4 runs itself.
